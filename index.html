LEAGUE RULES

Official regulations · Season 1

MATCH FORMAT

  • All matches are played on EA FC Mobile.
  • Each match consists of standard-length halves.
  • Matches must be played within 72 hours of being scheduled.
  • Both players must agree on a match time via the group chat.

POINTS SYSTEM

  • Win — 3 Points
  • Draw — 1 Point each
  • Loss — 0 Points
  • Tiebreaker: Goal Difference → Goals For → Head-to-Head

RESULT SUBMISSION

  • The winning player must post a screenshot of the final score in the group chat.
  • The admin will then confirm and log the result.
  • Disputed results will be reviewed by the admin panel.

CONDUCT

  • No abuse, harassment, or unsportsmanlike behaviour.
  • Rage-quitting before 85 minutes will count as a loss (0-3).
  • Consistent no-shows may result in removal from the league.
  • Admin decisions are final.

PRIZES & STANDINGS

  • Top 3 players at season end will be recognised on the Mettlestate platform.
  • Prizes (if applicable) will be announced per season.
  • The admin reserves the right to update rules between seasons.
